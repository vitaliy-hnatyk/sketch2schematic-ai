# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project participants should be respectful, constructive, patient, and welcoming. Technical disagreement is expected; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and project-related public communication.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer. Reports will be reviewed in good faith, with attention to safety, privacy, and proportional action.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or ban accounts when necessary to protect the project community.
